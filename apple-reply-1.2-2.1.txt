Guideline 1.2:

- Age rating: 18+ in App Store Connect.

- Terms (EULA): no one can sign in until they tick "I agree to the Terms of Use (EULA) and the Community Guidelines. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users." Our Terms of Use (sayit.taliferro.tech/terms, linked from the sign-in screen) and the Community Guidelines (Me > Community Guidelines) say offending content is removed and its author banned.

- Filtering: every new post's text and photo are screened automatically. Posts rated offensive are never shown; sensitive posts are covered with a warning.

- Flag: on any post, tap ••• to open it, then the More button at the top right > Report Post. On a comment, press and hold > Report Comment.

- Block: in the same More menu > Block [name], or press and hold a comment > Block. Blocked people's posts, comments and messages disappear immediately. Manage them in Me > Blocked People.

- Remove from feed: More > Hide Post removes it from the feed immediately.

- 24-hour action: every report emails our moderation inbox at once, with one-tap actions to remove the post and ban its author. Removed posts disappear for everyone, and banned accounts can no longer post, comment or message. We act on reports within 24 hours.

- Contact: Me > Contact Support, the Community Guidelines page, and every report screen show info@taliferro.tech.
