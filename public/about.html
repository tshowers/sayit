About SayIt

SayIt is a community board for real businesses and the people who need them. You say what you need or what you're selling. The people who can help say they're interested. Then you talk it over, one-on-one.

Why SayIt

Most social media is built around followers: the more you have, the more you're heard. That works for entertainment and not so well for getting something done. If you need a bookkeeper, a roofer or a supplier of compostable packing, you don't need an audience. You need the handful of people who can actually help, and a quick way to reach each other.

On SayIt every post is a need or an offer, and the button that matters is I'm interested. Tapping it puts the post in your inbox and lets you message the person who wrote it, privately.

What you can do

Post what you're looking for or selling

Add a photo or a link. Links show a preview, and YouTube and Vimeo videos play right in the post.

Find people by organization

People who name the same business on their profile show up together, so you can see who's posting from an organization.

Reply one-on-one

Your interest inbox keeps every post you're following up on in one place.

Web and iPhone

SayIt is free. It works in any browser at sayit.taliferro.tech, and the iPhone and iPad app is coming soon. It's one account either way: start on the web and pick up in the app, or the other way around.

Safety

Every post is screened automatically before it appears. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and content that breaks the community guidelines is removed and its author banned. See the community guidelines.
