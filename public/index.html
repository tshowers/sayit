Say what you need. Hear from people who can help.

SayIt is a community board for real businesses and the people who need them. Post what you’re looking for or what you’re selling. People who can help tap I’m interested, and you message each other one-on-one. No followers, no noise. It’s free.

How it works

Three steps, then you’re talking.

  1. 1

    Say it

    Post what you’re looking for or what you’re selling, with a photo, a video or a few words.

  2. 2

    Get found

    Businesses and neighbors who can help see your post and tap I’m interested.

  3. 3

    Talk it over

    Message each other one-on-one and work out the details. No public threads.

Every post is screened automatically, and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
