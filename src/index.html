Say what you need. Get found.

SayIt is a community board for real businesses and the people who need them. Post what you're looking for or what you're selling, and talk it over one-on-one with the people who are interested.

Start on the web Coming soon to iPhone & iPad

How it works

  1. Say itPost what you're looking for or what you're selling, with a photo or a link if it helps.
  2. Get foundPeople who can help tap I'm interested, and it lands in your inbox.
  3. Talk it overMessage each other one-on-one and get it done. No followers, no noise.

One account, web or iPhone

Start here on the web and pick up in the iPhone app, or the other way around. SayIt is free. Every post is screened automatically and reports are reviewed within 24 hours.
